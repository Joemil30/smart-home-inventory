Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026 — cloud account features described below are available only on enabled builds.

Your kitchen, your choice. You can use Stocked on this device without an account. When accounts are enabled, you can choose a private cloud kitchen or a shared household. Signing in does not upload your original device kitchen. Sharing selected food and recipes requires an explicit copy. Stocked does not sell kitchen data or use advertising trackers.

1. What is stored, and where

On your device, always

All of it is held in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) on the device itself. The original device kitchen works offline. Optional cloud kitchens and integrations send the data described below to their services.

  • Food items: names, categories, quantities, prices you enter, expiry dates, which shelf or drawer they're on, and their position on a shelf photo
  • Photos you take — of products, meals, and of your own shelves and drawers
  • Shopping lists, stores, aisle layouts and per-store names you set
  • Purchase history: what you bought, where, when, and how often
  • Recipes you save or import, your ratings, and how many times you've cooked each
  • Meal plans, settings, and a log of actions used for the Insights screen

Accounts and private or shared cloud kitchens

On account-enabled builds, Supabase stores your account email, authentication information, household membership, and records you create or explicitly copy into a cloud kitchen. Cloud records include food, the product catalog, saved recipes, shopping, meal plans and kitchen layout. Device API keys, local appearance settings and local activity history are not automatically uploaded by this system.

Your private kitchen is accessible through the app only to your signed-in account. Shared household records are accessible to its current members. The household owner cannot read another member's private kitchen. The app operator and infrastructure provider have administrative access: this is access-controlled storage, not end-to-end encryption. Supabase and the email delivery provider also process operational logs and authentication emails.

Removing a family member prevents new server access but cannot recall offline or exported copies. Opening a cloud kitchen requires an online access check; edits made offline in an already-open kitchen wait for reconnection.

The older code-based Family sync setup is separate from account-based cloud kitchens. Do not connect its permissive legacy schema to the account database.

Only if you turn on notifications

A push subscription token is stored in your own Supabase project so reminders can reach your phone.

2. Features that send data out

Every one of these is optional, and each is listed with exactly what leaves the device.

FeatureWhat is sentWhere
Receipt scanning, shelf photos, AI meal ideas, habit summary, list translation The photo or text you chose, plus the item names involved Google Gemini, or OpenRouter if you pick it — using your own API key
Barcode lookup and product photosThe barcode or product name Open Food Facts
Recipe photos and suggestionsA dish or ingredient nameTheMealDB
Recipe importA request to the recipe page you pastedThat website
Family sync and notificationsYour inventory and lists Your own Supabase project

On-device date scanning uses Tesseract, which runs entirely on your phone — no photo of a printed date is ever uploaded.

When you use one of these, that provider's own privacy policy applies to the request: Google, OpenRouter, Open Food Facts, Supabase.

3. What we never do

  • No advertising analytics; infrastructure providers may keep operational logs
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or renting personal data; chosen integrations and household sharing send data as described above
  • No account required to use the app

4. Your data, your control

  • Export — Settings → Backup writes a complete copy you can keep or move elsewhere.
  • Delete — remove any item at any time. Deleting the app, or clearing its site data, erases everything on the device.
  • Delete your account — on enabled builds, Account → Account security → Delete my account removes your sign-in and private cloud records. Transfer ownership first if you own a household with other members. Shared contributions remain with that household without your author ID. The last member's deletion removes its shared kitchen. Previously downloaded copies, device data and provider backup/log retention are separate.
  • Stop syncing — disconnect at any time; the data stays on your phone.

For help with account access, exports or deletion, contact the app operator below. Cloud synchronization is not an independent backup. Keep a private recovery export before clearing browser storage, especially if edits have not yet synced.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

6. Data sources and credits

Product names, barcodes and photos come from Open Food Facts, used under the ODbL 1.0, with photos under CC BY-SA 3.0. Recipe photos and suggestions come from TheMealDB. Waste comparisons use the UNEP Food Waste Index household range.
