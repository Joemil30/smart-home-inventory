Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 August 2026

The short version. Everything lives on your own phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you switch on a feature that needs to, and each of those is off until you turn it on. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone.

1. What is stored, and where

On your device, always

All of it is held in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) on the device itself. The app works fully offline. None of this reaches a server unless you turn on Family sync.

  • Food items: names, categories, quantities, prices you enter, expiry dates, which shelf or drawer they're on, and their position on a shelf photo
  • Photos you take — of products, meals, and of your own shelves and drawers
  • Shopping lists, stores, aisle layouts and per-store names you set
  • Purchase history: what you bought, where, when, and how often
  • Recipes you save or import, your ratings, and how many times you've cooked each
  • Meal plans, settings, and a log of actions used for the Insights screen

Only if you turn on Family sync

Family sync is off by default. When you enable it, the data above is copied to a Supabase project that you create and control — not to us. We operate no server and hold no copy of your data. If you later add sign-in, your email address is stored by Supabase to identify your account.

Only if you turn on notifications

A push subscription token is stored in your own Supabase project so reminders can reach your phone.

2. Features that send data out

Every one of these is optional, and each is listed with exactly what leaves the device.

FeatureWhat is sentWhere
Receipt scanning, shelf photos, AI meal ideas, habit summary, list translation The photo or text you chose, plus the item names involved Google Gemini, or OpenRouter if you pick it — using your own API key
Barcode lookup and product photosThe barcode or product name Open Food Facts
Recipe photos and suggestionsA dish or ingredient nameTheMealDB
Recipe importA request to the recipe page you pastedThat website
Family sync and notificationsYour inventory and lists Your own Supabase project

On-device date scanning uses Tesseract, which runs entirely on your phone — no photo of a printed date is ever uploaded.

When you use one of these, that provider's own privacy policy applies to the request: Google, OpenRouter, Open Food Facts, Supabase.

3. What we never do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data
  • No account required to use the app

4. Your data, your control

  • Export — Settings → Backup writes a complete copy you can keep or move elsewhere.
  • Delete — remove any item at any time. Deleting the app, or clearing its site data, erases everything on the device.
  • Delete your account — if you created one for sync, Settings has a delete button that removes your sign-in and leaves every household. If you were the last member, that household's synced copy is deleted too.
  • Stop syncing — disconnect at any time; the data stays on your phone.

These cover the rights the UK/EU GDPR and the CCPA give you over access, portability and erasure. Because we hold no copy of your data, you exercise them directly in the app rather than by asking us.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

6. Data sources and credits

Product names, barcodes and photos come from Open Food Facts, used under the ODbL 1.0, with photos under CC BY-SA 3.0. Recipe photos and suggestions come from TheMealDB. Waste comparisons use the UNEP Food Waste Index household range.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the date above changes and the app will say so before the new behaviour starts.
